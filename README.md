# Mechanical Mechanism Design & Fabrication
**CAD Modelling | Motion Studies | Mechanical Design | Prototype Development**

## 1. Project Overview

This project focuses on the design and development of three mechanical mechanisms: Can Crusher, Robert Mechanism, and Cam-Roller Slotting Mechanism.

The mechanisms were modelled using SolidWorks to understand component design, assembly relationships, and mechanical motion.

## 2. Objectives

- Design and model different mechanical mechanisms.
- Understand the working principles of mechanical linkages.
- Develop 3D models and assemblies.
- Study component movement through motion studies.
- Gain practical experience in fabrication and assembly.
